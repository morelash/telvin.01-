Popular Destinations

Maasai Mara

Experience amazing wildlife and the famous Great Migration in one of Kenya's best safari destinations.

Diani Beach

Relax on beautiful white sandy beaches and enjoy the warm waters of the Indian Ocean.

Amboseli

See elephants and enjoy spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Mombasa

Discover coastal culture, historical sites, beaches and delicious Swahili food.

Mount Kenya

Enjoy hiking, beautiful landscapes and the breathtaking scenery around Africa's second-highest mountain.

Lake Nakuru

Enjoy beautiful scenery and discover birds, rhinos and other wildlife.
